# Visual assets

The hero image was generated specifically for SYNKRA with OpenAI ImageGen: an editorial sports photograph of an adult woman doing a controlled lunge in a daylight industrial gym, neutral concrete palette, negative space on the left for typography. Delivered as an optimized local WebP. It depicts no actual trainer or partner club.
